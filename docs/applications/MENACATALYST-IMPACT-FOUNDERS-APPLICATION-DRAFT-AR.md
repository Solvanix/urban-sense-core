## Section 4 — About your Startup

### 4.1 Brief description — maximum 200 words

> SENSE is an early-stage Palestinian tourism and commerce platform designed to connect places, stories, local experiences and products. The problem is not a lack of heritage or creative work; it is the fragmented path between a place, the information a visitor needs, the local person who can offer an experience, and the product a maker wants to sell. SENSE proposes one trusted journey: discover a destination, understand its story, plan a route, find a local experience, and support a maker through a product connected to that place. We are starting with a focused pilot around Al-Eizariya and related Palestinian destinations, while building the content, data model, verification workflow and public prototype needed for responsible expansion. The current product is in prototype/MVP development. It does not yet claim live bookings, payments, delivery, or verified commercial traction. Our goal is to test whether structured, accessible and locally reviewed digital content can turn casual passage into informed visits and measurable local opportunity.

## Section 5 — Impact & Innovation

### 5.1 Social, environmental or economic impact — maximum 100 words

> SENSE aims to create local economic and social value by making Palestinian destinations, experiences and products easier to discover and trust. The intended beneficiaries include local makers, small tourism providers, communities and visitors who currently face fragmented information and limited routes from attention to action. At this stage, we report prototypes and published content rather than claiming revenue or user impact. Our first measurement cycle will track verified stories and destinations, participating providers, accessibility information, visitor actions, referrals to local businesses and feedback from makers and users. The long-term goal is a transparent, locally reviewed system that helps more value remain with communities while improving how visitors understand and move through a place.

**Word count:** approximately 89 words.

### 5.2 What makes the solution innovative or different — maximum 100 words

> SENSE is not only a directory and not only an online shop. It connects five layers that are usually separated: place, story, route, local experience and product. Each item carries a verification status, source, rights information, accessibility notes and last-review date. This makes the platform more trustworthy than a collection of promotional listings, while allowing commerce to follow a meaningful local story rather than detach products from their makers and places. We are also designing the system so that tourism content, community review and future municipal collaboration can be added gradually without claiming operational services before they are tested.

**Word count:** approximately 90 words.

### 5.3 Target customer or beneficiary — maximum 100 words

> The primary beneficiaries are Palestinian makers, small tourism and hospitality providers, local communities and visitors looking for reliable destination information. The initial customer groups are local providers who need a structured way to present their stories and products, and institutions or destination partners that need better-organized cultural and tourism information. Visitors are the end users: they need to understand what they are seeing, how to plan a responsible route and how to support a local person or product. The first pilot focuses on a small, reviewable geography rather than trying to serve the whole country at once.

**Word count:** approximately 89 words.

### 5.4 Use of the $10,000 prize — maximum 100 words

We would reserve part of the budget for measurement, documentation and legal/rights review. We would not spend the prize on claiming nationwide scale before the pilot proves that the content, route and local-commerce journey work in practice.
